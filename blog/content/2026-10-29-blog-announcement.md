_You can add this blog to your feed reader to be alerted whenever new posts are published using `https://grapheneos.org/blog`._

We're excited to launch the official GrapheneOS blog - a dedicated space for longer-form updates, insights, and announcements about the project. 

Most of our users simply want a secure, privacy-preserving mobile experience, and should receive clear, straightforward communication about the OS they’re using. Our goal is to make staying informed effortless by providing a convenient way to receive news about improvements to GrapheneOS. Additionally, we want to share not just what's happening, but the reasoning behind our decisions and how new features strengthen privacy and security.

## What to Expect

This blog will host in-depth articles on relevant topics, development updates, and project announcements. We're building a more sustainable communication strategy that reduces our dependence on external platforms while ensuring all GrapheneOS users have consistent access to information with content delivered directly to their device without algorithmic filtering or platform restrictions.

## Looking Ahead 

Our social media presence and community channels will remain active. Rather than disappearing, those spaces will increasingly direct users to our forum discussions and blog posts, where content can go deeper, be streamlined, and remain easily accessible to everyone.

We're excited to use this space to share the reasoning behind our development decisions, explore privacy and security related topics, and keep our growing community informed on their own terms.
